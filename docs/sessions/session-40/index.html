9. Reflection Questions

  1. Which boundary protects the app from API changes?

    The service normalization boundary converts external records into the stable internal Country contract.

  2. Which evidence best proves user value?

    Focused tests and a walkthrough that exercise visible output, interaction, async recovery, and service outcomes.

  3. What makes the capstone maintainable after completion?

    Explicit ownership, documented tradeoffs, automated checks, and a prioritized roadmap grounded in known risks.

10. What Breaks If This Boundary Is Ignored?

  • Responsibilities drift across unrelated modules.
  • Safe changes become harder to verify and explain.
  • Future learners inherit coupling without its original reasoning.

11. What Engineering Concept Was Learned Today

The capstone is complete when its behavior works, its boundaries are defensible, and its decisions are supported by evidence.
